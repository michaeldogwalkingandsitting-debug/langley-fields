    Questions

    Can I really have the field to myself?

    Yes. Only one booking runs at a time, so nobody else is on the field during your slot.

    How many dogs can I bring?

    Tell us how many dogs are coming when you book. Details on dog numbers are coming soon.

    How secure is the fencing?

    The field has 1.8m fencing with a dig-guard along the base, and an airlock of two gates at the entrance so a dog can never go straight from the field to outside.

    How do I get in?

    After you book, you receive a code for the keypad padlock. It works for your slot only.

    Is there water and parking?

    Yes. There is a water tank with bowls and a parking area by the gate. Seating is provided.

    What if the weather is bad?

    The field stays open in rain. If the ground becomes unsafe, we close it and contact you to rebook your slot at no cost.

    Can I cancel or change my booking?

    Our cancellation policy is coming soon. Please let us know as early as you can if you need to change a booking.
